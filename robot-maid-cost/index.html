Robot maid cost: what chore robots cost to buy or subscribe

You can now order a robot that folds laundry, tidies up or picks socks off the floor. Almost all of them make you choose between paying once and paying every month. Here is what each one costs, and a calculator that shows which way is cheaper for you, and how it compares with paying a cleaner.

What the monthly fees pay for

  • Weave Isaac 1: if you buy it, the optional $99 a month sits on top of the $7,999 price. The $449 a month plan replaces the purchase.
  • Syncere Lume: the $2,699 robot needs an autonomy add-on to fold on its own: $679 once, or $79 a month. At those prices the one-off fee is cheaper after 9 months.
  • 1X NEO: $499 a month or $20,000. Renting is cheaper if you keep it less than about 3.4 years, before interest.

Questions to ask before you pay

  1. Does it still work if I stop paying the monthly fee? Some robots lose their "smart" features without it.
  2. Is a person watching or steering it remotely? Some home robots use remote operators for tasks they cannot yet do alone. Ask who sees your camera feed, and when.
  3. What happens if the company closes? Robots that rely on the maker's cloud can stop working. Ask whether it works offline.
  4. Is there a usage cap on loads, hours or tasks?
  5. What is the minimum term, and the cost of ending early?
  6. Who pays for repairs after the warranty?
