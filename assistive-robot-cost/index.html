Assistive robot cost: feeding robots, robot arms, bionic hands and companion robots

Robots that help disabled and older people eat, reach, grip or stay in touch are on sale now. Makers rarely show prices, and funding depends on where you live. Here is what we could find, who might pay, and a calculator for your real cost over the years.

Your cost over the years

Many assistive robots mix an upfront price with a monthly fee. Add both, take off what a scheme pays, and see the total.

Ask for an itemised quote

Makers often quote one figure for "the device". Ask them to list each part separately:

  1. The device itself, and any software or monthly fee.
  2. Fitting, training and therapist sessions.
  3. Delivery and set-up at home.
  4. Warranty length, and the price of repairs and batteries after it ends.
  5. Whether a trial or rental is possible before you commit.
