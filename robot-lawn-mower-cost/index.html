Robot lawn mower cost vs paying someone to mow

A robot mower costs from under $800 to over $4,000. A mowing service costs about $60 a visit in the US, or about £30 an hour in the UK. Here are the prices, the running costs makers mention less often (blades, batteries, 4G fees), and a calculator that shows how many seasons it takes for a robot to pay for itself.

What paying someone costs

  • US: most people pay about $60 a visit ($46 to $75), according to Lawn Love (Nov 2026). Angi puts the average at $123 a visit (Angi). The season runs about 24 weeks in the Northeast, 26 in the Midwest and up to 46 in the Southwest.
  • UK: grass cutting costs about £30 an hour, with cuts every fortnight from March to October (Checkatrade, May 2026). MyBuilder gives £12 to £50 a mow (MyBuilder).

Questions to ask before you buy

  1. Does it need 4G, and what does that cost after the free period? Can you fit your own SIM card?
  2. Does it work if the app or the company goes away?
  3. Is it covered against theft on your home insurance? Does it have an alarm and PIN lock?
  4. Is it wired or wire-free? Wire-free saves installation but needs good satellite or camera coverage.
  5. Can it handle your slopes and narrow passages? Check the maker's figures for your lawn.
