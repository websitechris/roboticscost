Questions to ask a dealer

  1. What does the rental include? Maintenance, consumables like brushes and pads, software, mapping, and support.
  2. What is the minimum term? Some monthly prices assume 36 months.
  3. Who maps the building, and what does re-mapping cost when you move things around?
  4. Does it need Wi-Fi or a camera? Ask what it records, and where that goes.
  5. What does a service call cost once the warranty ends?
