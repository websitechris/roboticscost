Robotaxi cost: Waymo vs Uber vs owning a car

Driverless taxis now run in several US cities. What does a ride cost, and could you sell your car and ride instead? Here are the published average fares, the real cost of owning a car, and a calculator that shows where the line sits for you.

Figures checked November 2026. Fares change with time of day, demand and city, so treat averages as a guide.

Tesla's fares are rising too. In March 2026 it raised robotaxi prices by up to 40%, less than a year after launch (The Driven, Mar 2026).

Robotaxis in the UK

  • Waymo said in January 2026 it aims to launch a fully driverless service in London by the fourth quarter of 2026 (Reuters via The Star, Jan 2026).
  • Uber and Wayve have opened a London waitlist for robotaxi trials in 2026 (The Next Web, Jun 2026).
  • The Department for Transport is bringing in permits that let driverless taxis run without a safety driver (same source).

No UK fares have been published yet. When they are, put them into the calculator below with your own car costs in pounds.

What owning a car really costs

AAA's 2026 study puts the average cost of owning and running a new car at $12,863 a year, or about $1,072 a month (AAA, Sep 2026). The biggest part is depreciation: cars lost an average of $4,422 a year in value. Fuel, insurance, finance, repairs and fees make up the rest.

If your car is older and paid off, your own cost is likely lower. Put your own figure into the calculator.

Robotaxi vs your own car: calculator

At the Waymo average of $19.69 and the AAA car cost, the break-even is about 13 rides a week. Two short trips a day, every day, still costs less than owning a new car.

What the sum leaves out

  • Long trips cost more. The averages are mostly short city rides. Weekend drives, airport runs and school runs add up fast.
  • Coverage. Robotaxis only run inside set areas of a few cities. Outside them you still need a car, a taxi or a lift.
  • Waiting. Tesla's average wait was 15 minutes in the Obi data, Waymo's under 6.
  • Selling your car. The money you get for it can be invested, which makes riding look a little better.
