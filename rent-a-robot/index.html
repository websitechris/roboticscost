Rent a robot: what robot rental costs, and when buying is cheaper

You can now rent robots by the day, month or year: humanoids for events, serving robots for restaurants, cleaning robots, cobots and home robots. Here are the published rental prices we could find, and a calculator that shows when renting stops making sense.

UK robot hire prices

One London hire firm gives these day rates for events (Robots of London, Jul 2025):

  • Basic service robot: £400 to £800 a day
  • Humanoid robot such as Pepper: £1,000 to £2,000 a day
  • Robot arm for a bar or barista stand: £2,000 to £5,000 a day

We found no published UK monthly lease prices for serving or cleaning robots. Ask for a written quote that lists what is included.

Rent or buy? Break-even calculator

Renting wins for short jobs. Keep the robot long enough and buying wins. This finds the month where the two cross, counting the value of money you keep in the bank while renting.

Rental often includes set-up, support and repairs that you would pay for separately if you bought. For a business, the full robot lease vs buy calculator adds tax, hire purchase and resale value, and robotics as a service explains paying per hour or per task.

Questions to ask a rental firm

  1. What is included? Delivery, set-up, training, repairs, software updates, insurance.
  2. What is the minimum term, and what does ending early cost?
  3. Is there a usage cap on hours, square metres or tasks?
  4. Can rent count towards buying it at the end?
  5. Who is liable if the robot damages property or hurts someone?
