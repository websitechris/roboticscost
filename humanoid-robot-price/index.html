Humanoid robot prices: what home robots cost to buy or rent

The first humanoid and home robots now have real price tags. Most come with a choice: pay once, or pay every month. Here is what each one costs, where the figure comes from, and when renting stops making sense.

Prices checked November 2026. Prices change fast and most of these robots are still on pre-order, so check the maker's own page before you pay a deposit.

For comparison, most people expect far lower prices. In a December 2025 survey of over 1,000 US adults, 68% said they would not pay more than $5,000 for a home robot (Altman Solon via Consulting.us).

Buy or rent? The 1X NEO example

NEO costs $20,000 to buy or $499 a month to rent. Renting costs less than buying if you keep it for less than:

  • 41 months (about 3.4 years), if you ignore interest.
  • 49 months (about 4.1 years), if your money could otherwise earn or save you 10% a year. Money you keep in the bank is worth something, so renting looks a little better.

Over five years, renting adds up to $29,940, about $24,000 in today's money at 10%. That is more than the $20,000 price, so if you are sure you will keep it five years, buying is cheaper. But a rental lets you hand it back if it disappoints, or swap to a better model. With a first-generation product, that is worth paying for.

Two things this sum leaves out, because 1X has not published them: what buyers pay for repairs and software after any warranty, and what a used NEO will be worth. Both push the answer towards renting.

Questions to ask before you pay a deposit

  1. What does the monthly fee include? Repairs, software updates, insurance, delivery?
  2. Is there a usage cap? Buyers ask whether "$499 a month" has limits on hours or tasks.
  3. Is a person controlling it remotely? NEO uses remote operators to do and learn tasks it cannot yet do alone (Engadget). Ask who sees your home's cameras, and when.
  4. What is the minimum term, and what does it cost to hand it back early?
  5. What happens if the company fails? Robots that rely on the maker's cloud can stop working. Ask whether it works offline.
  6. Can I buy it at the end of the rental, and for how much?
  7. Is the deposit refundable, and when will it actually ship?
